## 2. Two Explicit Skills I Need to Strengthen

Comparing my current portfolio ([Idea Thinker Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/7pKC9IzbJfw51wonL2CNi1/IDEA-THINKER?t=N3qugy3usVNQrT7o-1), [Offline TV Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/1dhaIImEsfI7EQQCd9t8kF/OFFLINE-TV?node-id=0-1&p=f&t=QdVnJVRqyrqCE2mm-0), [The Reckoner](https://reckoner-son-of-soil.base44.app/), and my [6 itch.io visual novels/games](https://jiviteshdjollygamers.itch.io/)) against the TikTok (`J01`) and Notion/NYT NYC (`J03–J04`) postings revealed **two specific skills I need to strengthen**:

1. **Skill to Strengthen 1 — Mobile Design-System & Component Token Discipline (iOS HIG & Android Material 3):** My existing Figma files (*Idea Thinker* and *Offline TV*) were built as screen-by-screen visual layouts rather than reusable component libraries. I need to strengthen my ability to build tokenized Figma components with consistent `44x44pt` minimum touch targets, auto-layout spacing tokens, and WCAG AA contrast ratios that adhere to iOS Human Interface Guidelines and Android Material Design.
2. **Skill to Strengthen 2 — AI Error/Recovery State Design & Task-Based Peer Usability Testing:** While my itch.io games and web prototypes show happy-path branching choices, I need to strengthen my skill in designing explicit **empty, loading, and weak/unhelpful AI response recovery states** and testing them with real peer users (observing where someone hesitates without hints and revising the interface based on that evidence).

## 3. Concrete One-Week Step Executed: One Interface, One Peer Task & Recorded Change

To make my one-week step concrete, I chose **one specific interface**—the ***Idea Thinker* Guided Thinking (MCQ Mode) Flow** ([Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/7pKC9IzbJfw51wonL2CNi1/IDEA-THINKER?t=N3qugy3usVNQrT7o-1), captured in [`/screenshots/idea-thinker.png`](/screenshots/idea-thinker.png))—ran **one peer usability task**, and **recorded the concrete design change** resulting from that check:

| Step | What Was Done & Observed This Week |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Chosen Interface** | **Idea Thinker — Guided Thinking (MCQ Mode) & Result Screen** ([`/screenshots/idea-thinker.png`](/screenshots/idea-thinker.png)). |
| **2. Single Peer Usability Task Run** | Asked a classmate reviewer to start from a blank story/app idea on the *Idea Thinker* home screen, enter *Guided Thinking (MCQ Mode)*, and explain what they would click if the generated AI suggestion felt too generic or unhelpful—without any verbal hints from me. |
| **3. Observed Friction** | In the original 8-screen Figma flow (`screenshots/idea-thinker.png`), once a user reached the end of *Guided Thinking (MCQ Mode)*, there was **no inline action to refine a single constraint, retry with a narrower prompt, or switch directly into *Movie Twist Mode***—forcing the user to click all the way back to the home screen and start over from scratch. |
| **4. Concrete Change Recorded** | Added a **3-State AI Recovery & Mode-Switch Specification** directly to the *Idea Thinker* case study in [`docs/work.md`](../work.md) and [`docs/plan.md`](../plan.md):<br>1. **Empty-Prompt Starter Chips:** Added 3 tappable starter chips (*"Sci-Fi Game Mechanic"*, *"Campus Utility App"*, *"Plot Twist Generator"*) so a user never faces a cold blank box.<br>2. **Visible Loading Step Indicator:** Shows *"Mapping 3 angles from your MCQ choices..."* so the user knows what the tool is doing.<br>3. **Inline "Not Helpful? Refine or Twist" Recovery Bar:** Added two persistent buttons below every generated idea—**"Keep Topic, Change Tone (Retry 1 Step Back)"** and **"Send This Idea into Movie Twist Mode"**—eliminating the dead-end reset to the home screen. |
