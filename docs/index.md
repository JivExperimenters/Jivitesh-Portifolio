# I design interactions that help ideas move

I am exploring AI product and interaction design for consumer apps and creative tools. I am especially interested in the moment when a person has an idea but does not know how to develop it: how can a product give enough structure to help without taking creative control away?

<div class="vp-doc" style="max-width: 720px; margin: 0 auto; text-align: left">

My current direction grows from two things I keep returning to: designing interfaces and building interactive stories and games. I want to connect those interests through clear user flows, understandable system states, and prototypes people can actually try.

This site is a working portfolio, not a claim that every idea here is finished or user-tested. I label existing artifacts separately from proposed work and planned checks.

## What I want a reviewer to notice

- I am choosing a direction based on the actual work described in a graduate AI product-design posting, not only on an interest in AI.
- My current projects show interface concepts, interactive logic, and the ability to publish prototypes; they do not yet show documented user research or a tested AI recovery flow.
- My next design question is specific: can someone tell whether Idea Thinker is waiting, has no result, or needs a revised prompt—and can they recover without my explanation?

## Review route

Start with the [career research](./research/career): open the TikTok source, find the request for user-centered AI interaction and logic mapping, and follow that evidence to the proposed Idea Thinker response states and planned check. The check is not complete yet; there are no participant results to claim.

The full two role cards and personal reflection are submitted privately in Canvas. This public site focuses on suitable professional goals, research evidence, and portfolio proof.
