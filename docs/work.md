# Selected work I can show

These projects are examples of what I have made so far, not a claim that every artifact has been tested or meets production standards. I am choosing examples that connect to my interest in AI product interaction, mobile flows, creative tools, and interactive experiences.

## Idea Thinker — guided ideation concept

[Open my Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/7pKC9IzbJfw51wonL2CNi1/IDEA-THINKER?t=N3qugy3usVNQrT7o-1)

This is the project I want to develop into my focused AI product case study. The existing screens explore Guided Thinking, Movie Twist, and word-game modes. For me, the central design question is not “How do I add AI?” but “How can a guided interaction help someone move from a vague idea to a next step without obscuring what the tool is doing?”

**Current state:** Existing Figma concept. The empty/loading/unhelpful response states and peer test described in the [research plan](./research/career) are proposed work, not completed functionality or observed results.

## Offline TV — media hub flows

[Open my Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/1dhaIImEsfI7EQQCd9t8kF/OFFLINE-TV?node-id=0-1&p=f&t=QdVnJVRqyrqCE2mm-0)

I use this multi-screen concept to show my interest in navigation and organizing multiple kinds of content. It is useful supporting evidence for interface planning, but I have not presented it as evidence of a finished mobile design system or a user-tested navigation model.

## AppAssemblers — AI-assisted prototype

[Open AppAssemblers](https://app-assemblers.lovable.app/chat/3024b72a-955d-43df-a1ad-d493237cf2c7)

This is one of my experiments with an AI-assisted app-building workflow. It helps show that I explore ideas in working tools and shareable prototypes. It does not establish that Idea Thinker has a connected, reliable AI service.

## Interactive storytelling and games

[Open my itch.io collection](https://jiviteshdjollygamers.itch.io/)

Interactive stories and games are a continuing source of interest for me because they make state, choice, feedback, and progression visible. This work supports my creative-interaction perspective; it is adjacent evidence, not a substitute for research and usability evidence for a product-design role.

## Research as part of design

My [IS117 T-shirt research](https://github.com/JivExperimenters/T-Shirt) gave me practice distinguishing what I observed on reference sites from what I inferred and then proposing something checkable. I want to apply that method to my own work without treating competitor observations as proof of what Idea Thinker users need.
