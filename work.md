# Selected work I can show (with In-Repo Screenshots)

These projects are examples of what I have made so far, not a claim that every artifact has been tested or meets production standards. Following peer feedback in [Issue #4](https://github.com/JivExperimenters/Jivitesh-Portifolio/issues/4), I embedded screenshots of every project directly in this repository and prioritized **public, no-login links** so reviewers can inspect my work with zero sign-in friction.

## 1. Idea Thinker — guided ideation concept (Figma)

[Open public Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/7pKC9IzbJfw51wonL2CNi1/IDEA-THINKER?t=N3qugy3usVNQrT7o-1)

![Idea Thinker Figma Wireframes](/screenshots/idea-thinker.png)

This is the project I am developing into my focused AI product case study. The existing screens explore *Guided Thinking (MCQ Mode)*, *Movie Twist Mode*, and *Mind Games (Wordle and Crossword idea generators)*. For me, the central design question is not "How do I add AI?" but "How can a guided interaction help someone move from a vague idea to a next step without obscuring what the tool is doing?"

**Current state:** Existing Figma concept. The empty/loading/unhelpful response states and peer test described in the [research plan](./research/career) are proposed work, not completed functionality or observed results.

I use this 10-screen concept (*Home, Movies, Education, Settings, Music, Games,* and *Pictures*) to show my interest in navigation hierarchy and organizing multiple content categories.
